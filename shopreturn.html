ShopReturn

Stop losing money on cores and warranty parts. Every core charge gets a return deadline, every return gets a photo of the signed slip, and every vendor credit gets checked off, so nothing sits in the back bin until it expires.

  1. Tap the red AC compressor core. It's past its return date.
  2. Tap Mark Returned, snap the return slip, and save. Then mark the turbo core Credited.
  3. Open Vendors to see who still owes you money, with a list you can send their rep.

Every part, vendor, repair order, vehicle and dollar amount here is made up. Use the + button to log a new core. Drag the sheet down or flick it to close.
