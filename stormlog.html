StormLog

Proof of service for commercial snow contractors. Crews log every plow and salt visit in about 30 seconds. When a slip-and-fall claim shows up, you pull that lot's evidence packet in one tap.

  1. Tap a red site, like Lakeview Dental.
  2. Tap Start Visit, take the before and after photos, then Finish.
  3. Open Packets to see the claim-ready report. Drag the sheet down or flick it to close.

Every site, crew member, photo and weather reading here is made up. In the real app, weather comes from the nearest National Weather Service station and photos come from the crew's camera. StormLog documents the work you did; it is not legal advice and does not promise any outcome in a claim.
